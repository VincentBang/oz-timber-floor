FAQs

Hardwood timber flooring questions

These answers help customers narrow the hardwood category before they choose a range or ask for supply and installation help.

What does hardwood timber flooring mean in Sydney projects?

Hardwood timber flooring is a broad category covering real timber flooring options such as solid hardwood boards and engineered timber products with genuine timber surfaces.

Should I choose solid timber or engineered timber?

That depends on the site, acoustic requirements, subfloor condition, design brief and how much installation flexibility is needed. Engineered timber often suits apartments and premium interiors, while solid timber remains strong for traditional hardwood projects.

Can Oz Timber Floor help with Australian species options?

Yes. Oz can discuss hardwood timber flooring options in oak, Blackbutt, Spotted Gum and other Australian species depending on the product range and project brief.
