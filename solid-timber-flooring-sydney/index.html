FAQs

Common questions

These answers help prepare a useful enquiry before product selection or installation planning.

Can I buy solid timber flooring supply-only?

Yes. Send the range or colour, approximate quantity and suburb so Oz Timber Floor can confirm current stock and supply options.

Can Oz Timber Floor install solid timber flooring?

Yes. For supply + install enquiries, include the suburb, approximate area, current floor type, access details and preferred range or colour.

Do I need floor preparation before solid timber flooring?

Some projects need floor preparation before installation. Floor flatness, moisture, existing flooring, trims and transitions should be reviewed before the job is confirmed.

How do I check stock or lead time?

Send the range, colour, approximate square metres and suburb. Oz Timber Floor can then confirm current availability and suitable next steps.
