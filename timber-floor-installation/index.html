Installation service

Good installation starts before the boards arrive

Flooring installation is a sequence of decisions: product choice, substrate condition, underlay or adhesive method, trims, skirting, expansion gaps, stairs, access and timing.

Oz Timber Floor positions installation as a practical trade service for homes, apartments, builders and commercial projects. The goal is to match the flooring choice to the room, not force every product into the same method.

Installation planning

Checks before installation day

Clear preparation helps avoid delays, finish problems and unnecessary changes once installers are on site.

Measure and product choice

Confirm areas, wastage allowance, product category, board direction and supply status.

Subfloor and moisture

Check flatness, stability, moisture concerns and old floor residue before committing to install.

Details and transitions

Access and protection

Project sequence

Prepare the property and installation sequence

Installation timing becomes clearer when access, removal, preparation and other renovation work are discussed together.

Before the site review

Share measurements, photos or plans, the current floor, preferred product and any known access or strata conditions.

Before flooring arrives

Confirm stock, batch, delivery access, storage needs and the latest product-specific installation requirements.

Before work starts

Coordinate furniture, removal, wet trades, painting, cabinetry and any floor levelling so the new floor is installed in the right sequence.

Before handover

Review trims, transitions, doors, stairs and protection of the finished floor while remaining work is completed.
