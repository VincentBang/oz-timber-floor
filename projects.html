Before you enquire

Flooring project questions

Can I request flooring similar to one of these projects?

Yes. Tell us which photograph or flooring style you like, your approximate floor area and your suburb. We can review suitable products and the installation scope; the exact product and availability still need confirmation.

Does Oz Timber Floor install herringbone and chevron flooring?

We can review herringbone and chevron installation enquiries. Product format, layout, preparation and site suitability should be confirmed together before work is booked.

Can floor levelling be included in my installation quote?

Ask us to include preparation in the scope review. Floor levels, the existing surface and the selected product determine what is needed; the final allowance may require a site assessment.

Can you supply and install flooring for a renovation?

Yes. Send your preferred flooring type, approximate area, site photos and whether old flooring needs to be removed. Include any stairs, trims or access requirements.

What information do you need to prepare a quote?

Your suburb, approximate area, preferred product or flooring style, current floor surface, photos and expected timing are a useful starting point. Mention stairs and whether you need supply only or supply and installation.
