FAQ

Questions about Oak Step

What type of flooring is Oak Step?

Oak Step is a laminate flooring range. Oz Timber Floor can help confirm current product details, stock and project suitability before ordering.

What should I compare first in Oak Step?

Start with colour, board format, thickness and current stock timing, then confirm installation suitability for the project.

Can Oz Timber Floor supply Oak Step without installation?

Yes. Send the preferred colour, quantity and suburb so stock, batch and lead time can be checked.

Can Oak Step be quoted with installation?

Yes. Include the floor area, current floor type, suburb, access and timing so preparation and installation scope can be reviewed.

Can Oz Timber Floor help confirm product information for Oak Step?

Yes. Oz Timber Floor can help confirm current product information, stock timing, preparation notes and any relevant ratings or certificates before ordering.

What should I check before ordering Oak Step?

Confirm stock, batch, colour, floor condition, trims, transitions and installation suitability before ordering.
