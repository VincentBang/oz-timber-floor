What affects the finish

The finished result depends on the timber, past coatings, damage, repairs and site access as much as the sanding process itself.

FAQs

Common questions

These answers help prepare a useful enquiry before site inspection or product selection.

Can every timber floor be sanded and polished?

No. The boards need enough usable thickness and must be stable enough to sand. Severe water damage, deep staining, movement or repeated past sanding may make replacement more practical.

Will sanding remove all marks and stains?

Not always. Surface wear often improves, but deep stains, pet damage, water marks and colour variation may remain or require board repairs.

Can you advise whether to restore or replace?

Yes. Send photos, the age of the floor if known, the room area and the result you want. Oz can discuss whether sanding, partial repairs or replacement should be considered.

Do I need to move furniture before sanding?

Access and furniture removal should be planned before work. Include photos and room details in the enquiry so the team can discuss preparation requirements.
