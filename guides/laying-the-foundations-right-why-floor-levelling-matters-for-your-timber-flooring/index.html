When it comes to timber flooring, the secret to its durability, appeal, and overall longevity lies in the preparation — and at the heart of this preparation is floor levelling.

At Oz Timber Floor, we understand this intricate process and strive to provide unparalleled floor levelling services to ensure your timber floors stand the test of time.

Here’s a closer look at why floor levelling is non-negotiable when thinking about timber flooring.

Learn more about our floor levelling services to help you lay the foundations the right way for your timber flooring.

Why is Floor Levelling Essential?

Floor levelling addresses localised surface variation before flooring installation. Review the exposed substrate, moisture and movement concerns before choosing a preparation method. A floor can slope and still be flat; the selected product's requirements determine the preparation needed.

Think of floor levelling as preparing and laying the groundwork for your timber floor to perform at its best. Preparation should be reviewed for the selected product. Structural movement, active cracking and moisture problems may need separate investigation rather than surface levelling alone.

The Process of Floor Levelling

At its core, floor levelling tackles the uneven concrete subfloor underneath your main floor. This process identifies and evens out disparities, ensuring a smooth surface. Our team utilises concrete grinding to decrease the size of these disparities and then fills in the remaining gaps with levelling concrete. This diligent approach ensures a smooth base, ready for your timber floor installation.

Why Opt for Professional Floor Levelling Services?

While there has been an influx of DIY methods and products for floor levelling, the expertise and precision of professionals cannot be overlooked.

By choosing us, you’re opting for:

Quality: Preparation should be checked against the selected product instructions, installation method and actual site conditions.

Affordability: While it may seem cost-effective to go the DIY route, professional services can prevent costly future damages.

Speed: Our trained team ensures a swift yet thorough job, reducing the downtime of your space.

Peace of Mind: Knowing that seasoned experts are handling your floor means you can rest easy about the end result.

Advantages of Opting for Floor Levelling

Affordability: Review the preparation scope and site condition before work is agreed. Structural or moisture problems may need separate investigation.

Durability: A properly prepared base helps support the selected flooring; final product and installation requirements still need to be checked.

Aesthetics: Review surface flatness, moisture and movement together. Levelling alone should not be assumed to prevent warping or every flooring problem.

Safety: Review uneven transitions and trip concerns as part of the site inspection before deciding the preparation scope.
