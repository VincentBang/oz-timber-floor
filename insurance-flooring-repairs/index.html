Insurance builders & repair projects

Flooring Repairs and Replacement for Insurance Projects

Oz Timber Floor carries out flooring work for insurance builders across Sydney. We also welcome enquiries from property owners and managers seeking a quote for an insurance-related flooring repair.

Our work includes small floor repairs, removal and replacement, and sanding or recoating of suitable timber floors. Share the flooring scope, property location and access requirements so we can discuss the job.

Flooring support for insurance builders

Send the flooring portion of your scope of works, the property suburb and your requested quotation date. Existing product information, approximate quantities and the work sequence can help us understand the requirements.

We can discuss the flooring work alongside the access arrangements, material selection and other trades involved. Where the requested work or site information needs clarification, the scope should be confirmed before scheduling.

For ongoing subcontracting enquiries, tell us the areas you cover and the types of flooring work you need. We can discuss our suitability and the information required for your contractor onboarding process.

Flooring work we can undertake

Small repairs and partial replacement

Ask about a localised damaged area or individual boards. The suitable approach depends on the flooring system, the condition of the surrounding area and the availability of appropriate replacement material.

Removal and replacement flooring

Where replacement is agreed, we can discuss removal of the existing flooring, the proposed replacement and the preparation and finishing items relevant to the job.

Floor removal and stripping

Sanding and recoating suitable timber floors

Sanding or recoating may be appropriate for an existing timber floor, depending on its construction, condition and finish. Suitability is considered before that work is included in the scope.

Timber floor sanding and polishing

Tenant and agency access coordination

If the property is occupied or the keys are held by a managing agent, we can arrange tenant appointments and agency key coordination as part of the agreed job arrangements.

Matching the existing floor

Existing flooring details, packaging or a spare board can help identify the product. Let us know what information is available when you enquire.

Matching can be affected by product availability and differences in colour, pattern, dimensions, finish and ageing. We will discuss the available options before the work is agreed. An exact match cannot always be achieved.

If a proposed substitution or a wider replacement area changes the agreed scope, it needs to be confirmed with the party authorising the work before proceeding.

Plan the flooring stage of the repair

Tell us about other work that needs to happen before or alongside the flooring. Access, the agreed site readiness, material availability and other trades can affect the sequence and dates.

If a leak or another cause of damage is still being addressed, include that in the brief so responsibilities and timing can be clarified with the builder or repair coordinator. Do not assume the flooring appointment also covers leak repairs, drying or specialist reporting.

Requesting a quote as a property owner or manager

You can contact Oz Timber Floor about an insurance-related flooring quote. Let us know whether an insurer, appointed builder or repair coordinator is already involved, and whether a flooring scope has been provided.

Our role is to discuss and quote the flooring work. A flooring quote does not confirm insurance coverage or approve a claim. Confirm the required authorisation with the insurer or appointed representative before arranging work.

How the flooring enquiry works

  1. Send the brief.

    Include the property suburb, flooring issue or existing scope, your role and the requested quotation date. A job reference is useful if you already have one.

  2. Clarify the flooring requirements.

    We will discuss the information available and whether a site visit is needed before the work can be quoted.

  3. Confirm the quote and authorised scope.

    Agree the work to proceed, the selected material where applicable and who can approve changes.

  4. Arrange access and the work sequence.

    Confirm the property contact, tenant or agency access arrangements and coordination with other trades.

  5. Complete the agreed flooring work.

    Any proposed additional work or change in scope is discussed with the appropriate contact before proceeding.

Questions about insurance-related flooring work

Do you work for insurance builders?

Yes. We undertake flooring work for insurance builders. Send the flooring scope, property suburb and requested quotation date so we can discuss the project.

Can I request a quote before my claim is approved?

You can enquire about a flooring quote. Let us know the current stage and whether an insurer or builder has requested it. The quote does not confirm cover or authorise repairs; the required approval must be confirmed separately.

Can you replace only the damaged boards?

A partial repair or replacement may be possible. It depends on the flooring system, the affected area and whether suitable replacement material is available. We can discuss the practical options before agreeing the scope.

Can you match discontinued flooring?

We can discuss the options using the existing product information or available samples. A discontinued product may no longer be obtainable, and differences in appearance or dimensions can limit matching. We cannot promise an exact match.

Can you coordinate access with a tenant or property manager?

Yes. Tenant appointments and agency key coordination can be arranged. Tell us who should manage access and agree the details before attendance.

What happens if the work required changes?

Changes to the agreed work need to be discussed and authorised before additional work proceeds. Tell us who is responsible for scope decisions so queries reach the appropriate person.

What information should I send first?

Include your contact details and role, the property suburb, the flooring issue or scope, the requested quotation date and relevant access or scheduling requirements. Tell us if you have photographs or documents, and we can discuss how to provide them. There is no need to send a full insurance policy or unrelated personal information with the first enquiry.
